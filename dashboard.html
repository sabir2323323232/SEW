Earning Sources (Kamai Ke Zariye)

Step 1: Design Banayein (1 Day)

Sabse pehle aapko ek design uthana hai aur ose 1 din me behtareen tariqe se banana hai.

Step 2: Upload & Live Voting

Dusre din aapna design upload karenge, jiske baad live voting start ho jayegi.

Step 3: Top 10 & Boli (Selling)
